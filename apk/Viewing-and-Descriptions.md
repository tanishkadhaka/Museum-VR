# Visiting paintings and artifacts

Paintings: clicking a painting moves you to its viewing spot. Repeated clicks also only travel. Paintings have no description data, description panels or narration.

Artifacts: click from a distance to move to the viewing spot. Click again nearby to open the description and synchronized narration. You can also walk there before clicking.

Walking remains available through WASD on desktop and the existing left-stick movement on Quest. Moving away closes artifact panels and stops narration. Physical Quest 3S testing remains pending.
