# Synchronized exhibit audio guide

Clicking a distant exhibit travels to its viewing spot without opening the description or audio. Click again nearby to open the description and start narration. You can also walk there yourself before clicking. Each exhibit has three sections: History, Look Closer, and Why It Matters. The displayed text advances when its matching audio section finishes.

- Previous / Next changes the text and matching audio together. During paused playback, navigation keeps the audio paused at the beginning of the new section.
- Pause / Resume preserves your place in the recording. Replay restarts the current section after it has finished.
- Closing the card, opening another exhibit, entering inspection, returning to the entrance, or hiding the panel stops the recording. Leaving the app pauses playback.
- The playback timer and progress bar describe the current section. Unavailable Previous / Next buttons are dimmed and cannot be selected.

All eight exhibits have three matching English audio recordings, generated with the installed macOS Samantha synthetic voice. Files are included in the APK and work offline. Source scripts and audio are in Assets/HeritageVR/Narration. The Android build targets Meta Quest 3S, Quest 3 and Quest 2. Physical Quest 3S testing remains pending.
