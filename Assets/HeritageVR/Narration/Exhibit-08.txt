Long before people built museums, Allosaurus lived in Jurassic landscapes. The Natural History Museum places it around 156 to 144 million years ago. This large meat-eating dinosaur walked on powerful hind legs and carried a long balancing tail.

A skeleton tells its story through relationships between bones. Look at the skull, rib cage, short forelimbs and tail. These structures help scientists investigate how an extinct animal moved and lived, even though its behaviour cannot be watched directly.

Allosaurus is special because it lets us explore a vanished ecosystem through physical evidence. This tabletop skeleton is greatly reduced in scale. The particular specimen and museum attribution of this digital model have not been verified.
