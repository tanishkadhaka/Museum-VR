A tall vessel changes the way we look at pottery. Follow the outline from the small mouth, across the shoulder and down the narrow body. A simple silhouette records decisions made by a maker.

Ancient ceramic containers served many purposes, including storage and transport. Archaeologists compare shapes, clay, decoration and excavation context to understand them. Shape alone is not enough to give an object a secure historical identity.

This display is special as a lesson in looking carefully. Compare its bands and proportions with the two wider vessels. The model is presented as a tall ceramic vessel; a specific archaeological type, workshop and date have not been confirmed.
