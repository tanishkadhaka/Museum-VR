Before modern packaging, pottery was part of the infrastructure of daily life. In the ancient Mediterranean, amphorae and related containers stored and carried products such as wine and oil. Ordinary vessels connected homes, workshops and trading routes.

This digital vessel has two handles, a narrow neck and a rounded body. Think about lifting, pouring and storing rather than decoration alone. A container’s shape can help us ask how people used it.
