This seated ruler is identified as King Khasekhemwy, also known as Khasekhem. The Ashmolean’s limestone statue comes from Hierakonpolis and belongs to Egypt’s Second Dynasty. It takes us close to the beginnings of royal stone sculpture.

The tall White Crown, compact throne and enveloping robe create a controlled, enduring image. Instead of showing a moment of everyday activity, the sculptor presents kingship as something solid and lasting.

Its importance lies in its great age and the early royal tradition it preserves. Compare the front with the side: the body and throne form a closely joined shape. This digital study represents the statue; it is not the ancient object itself.
