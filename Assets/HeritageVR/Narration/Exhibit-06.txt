A storage jar invites us to imagine the work of keeping a household supplied. Pottery containers belonged to the practical world of making, carrying and preserving goods. Their value was often measured through repeated use rather than precious materials.

Look at this model’s repeated ribs and the stand that supports its rounded body. Compare the broad profile with the narrower vessel nearby. These visible details make its construction and display worth examining.

Its importance in this gallery is the attention it brings to ordinary material culture. Useful objects can tell stories about skill, care and daily routines. The stand is part of the displayed model; its original use, date and origin remain unconfirmed.
